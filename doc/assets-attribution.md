Dork Core uses the existing Dorkcoin 13.2.7 application logo from
`src/qt/res/icons/bitcoin.png`. System icons, installer artwork, and the
documentation logo are resized or format-converted derivatives of that asset.
The inherited `bitcoin` asset filenames are kept to preserve build references;
they do not identify the software or network.
